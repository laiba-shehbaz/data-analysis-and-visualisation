# Findings Summary - ShopEase 2025 Orders

**Dataset:** self-created, 1,235 raw rows (generated by `generate_dataset.py`), 1,194 rows after cleaning.
**Total revenue analysed:** Rs 6.58M from 1,194 orders.

## Finding 1 - Sales are strongly seasonal
November is the best month with **Rs 966K**, which is
**1.9 times** the average of the other eleven months (Rs 510K).
November and December together bring **26%** of the whole year's revenue.
*Action:* stock up and plan advertising before the November sale period.

## Finding 2 - Revenue depends on a few categories
**Home & Kitchen** earns the most (**29.2%** of revenue) and the top two categories together earn
**54%**. Note that Fashion receives the most orders, but each order is worth only Rs 5,172 on average, against Rs 8,043 for Home & Kitchen.
*Action:* keep high-value categories well stocked and promote them first.

## Finding 3 - Discounts raise basket size
Orders with **30% discount** contain **1.97 units** on average, compared with
**1.36 units** at 0% discount. The average order value is Rs 5,145
at 30% and Rs 5,562 at 0%, so extra units do not always make up for the price cut.
*Action:* use deep discounts selectively (for example, to clear stock) instead of on every product.

## Finding 4 - Customer satisfaction differs between cities
**Faisalabad** has the highest average rating (**4.10**) and **Peshawar** the lowest
(**3.68**), a gap of 0.42 stars.
*Action:* investigate delivery time and service quality in the lowest-rated city.

## Limitations
* The data is synthetic, so the findings describe this dataset only and are not real market facts.
* Ratings exist for only part of the orders (92% of cleaned rows).
* The data shows association, not proof of cause (for example, discounts and the November season overlap).
